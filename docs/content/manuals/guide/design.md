# Design Features

1. [Core Applications](design/core.md) :
   A tightly knit core of applications provide access to most functionality.
2. [Common Options](design/options.md) :
   A standard set of command-line options provide consistency across core applications.
3. [Weighted Means](design/weighted_means.md) :
   Weighted mean pulse profiles, frequencies, and epochs are maintained.
4. [Cold Plasma](design/cold_plasma.md) :
   Dispersion and birefringence in the interstellar medium are differentiated from those arising in the ionosphere or solar corona.
5. [Baseline Removal](design/baseline_removal.md) :
   A variety of strategies and algorithms are available for this fundamental task.
6. [Configuration](design/configuration.md) :
   A variety of algorithms and options are supported.
7. [Evaluation of mathematical expressions](design/evaluation.md) :
   Convenient access to data attributes and derived values.
8. [Large File Support](design/large_file.md) :
   Optionally use custom virtual memory management to process large files.
