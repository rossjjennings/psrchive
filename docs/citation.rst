========

   Hotan, van Straten & Manchester (2004),
   "PSRCHIVE and PSRFITS: An Open Approach to Radio Pulsar Data Storage
   and Analysis", *Publications of the Astronomical Society of Australia*,
   **21**, 302.
   `doi:10.1071/AS04022 <https://doi.org/10.1071/AS04022>`_
